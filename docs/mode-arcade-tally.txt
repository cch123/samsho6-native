Bonus arithmetic summary: FINISH = table 6555e8 value (may be -1000); TIME = seconds left * 100;
LIFE = HUD life * 100, or * 200 when the life was exactly 200 at entry (label PERFECT);
STRAIGHT = 60000; TOTAL = sum, added to gameData+0x90 over 30 frames.
A v of exactly -1 terminates a phase without being added (reachable only with a non-table
582950 such as -1 under skip). Negative totals are paid like positive ones.
Duration without input: up to 30 frames per bonus present, 30 wait, 30 pay-out, 30 exit;
one frame is spent on each transition (the frame that returns -1 adds nothing).

9  Corrections to docs/mode-battle-branches.txt
-----------------------------------------------

  - Section 8: the pay-out sound (-1, 0x42) is requested on EVERY counted frame of phases 0..3
    and 5, not every second frame (the toggle word 582990 is cleared after each request).
  - Section 8: 58298c is not "the winner"; it is 1 when seat 0 has not reached its required wins.
  - Section 8: 1c7e00 latches the life of seat 582988 (the seat with more HUD life), not of a
    fixed seat; 582980 marks a 200-life round (bonus x200, label PERFECT).
  - Section 8 caller list: 157ac4 and 157be0 are the tail calls of the second (counter, kind
    0x10) award inside 157a58 / 157b50, not independent events.
  - Section 13: 1bc098 passes x from word[58224c + 4*mode] (115 / 571), y 0x10, depth -0x12d.
