Mode-dependent branches of the in-battle code (SLPS-25559)
==========================================================

Scope: every place the battle scene (battle thread 138f48, round dispatcher 143430/1435d8/143320,
actor/round initialisation, gauges, HUD, pause) reads the game-mode word or a derived predicate.
Purpose: allow the native battle (hard-limited to mode 3 by the `gameMode!=3` throws in
src/battle_round.cpp, src/round_control.cpp, src/match_ending.cpp) to admit ARCADE (1/2),
PRACTICE (9/10), SURVIVAL (0xb/0xc) and the attract demo (mode 0).
Method: static only. A linear constant-propagation scan of .text (0x100000..0x32cd70; the ELF
.text ends there, not at 0x440000) located every load/store whose effective address lies in
0x4c3808..0x4c38c0 and in 0x123a0b0..0x123a180, grouped by enclosing function; the battle-side
functions were then read. No Probe run was made.
Labels: OBSERVED = read from instructions/data at the cited address. INFERRED = interpretation.
UNKNOWN = not resolved. Delay slots are already folded into the pseudocode.
